# Prestige International Clinic – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Prestige International Polyclinic, also known as Prestige International Clinic, is a medical aesthetics and cosmetic clinic based in Al Barsha First, Dubai. The clinic provides aesthetic treatments, laser procedures, skin rejuvenation, injectables, and wellness services.

The redesigned homepage focuses on presenting the clinic’s expertise more clearly, helping visitors discover relevant treatments, and encouraging consultation enquiries through a more structured and professional experience.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Clearer Homepage Messaging and Stronger Calls to Action
- Replaced the vague headline with service-focused messaging that communicates the clinic’s aesthetic and cosmetic specialisation.
- Improved the visibility of the **Book a Consultation** and **WhatsApp Us** calls to action.
- Made the clinic’s core services easier to understand from the first section of the homepage.

### 2. Improved Trust Signals and Credibility
- Addressed the unfinished achievement counters that previously displayed empty statistics.
- Focused the trust section on verifiable clinic information, professional credentials, and treatment technology.
- Improved the presentation of the medical team and patient confidence elements without relying on unsupported figures or claims.

### 3. Better Treatment Discovery and Service Organisation
- Restructured treatment information into clearer service categories.
- Introduced a more organised treatment presentation to help visitors identify relevant procedures.
- Improved access to individual treatment information while reducing the prominence of extensive technical content that distracted from service discovery.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual presentation:** Created a more professional and consistent appearance suited to a medical aesthetics clinic.
- **Layout:** Improved content hierarchy and section organisation to make the homepage easier to scan.
- **User experience:** Made key consultation actions more prominent and simplified the treatment discovery journey.
- **Consistency:** Improved the presentation of treatment categories, credibility information, and calls to action.
- **Overall appearance:** Established a clearer homepage structure that communicates the clinic’s services and expertise more effectively.

## Project Objective

The redesign addresses three key weaknesses in the original homepage: unclear messaging, incomplete trust signals, and difficult treatment discovery. The result is a more focused homepage designed to help visitors understand the clinic’s services, build confidence, and take the next step toward a consultation.
