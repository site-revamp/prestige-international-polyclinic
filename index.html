Ask the Clinic ↗
How do I know which treatment is right for me?

Treatment suitability depends on your concerns, skin condition, medical history and personal goals. A consultation and appropriate assessment help determine which options may be suitable.

Can I book a consultation before choosing a treatment?

Yes. You can contact the clinic to arrange a consultation and discuss your goals before deciding on an appropriate treatment.

What laser technology does Prestige use?

The clinic publishes information about its Fotona SP Dynamis and Fotona StarWalker PQX platforms. Treatment selection depends on the individual concern and professional assessment.

Where is Prestige International Clinic located?
